🔬 Innovation: Eliminating the "Midas Touch" Problem

  • The Problem: Traditional dwell clicks trigger unintended clicks (~24% error rate) whenever the user merely glances or pauses their head.
  • Our Solution: The Intentional Dwell-to-Voice Gate arms the target in green upon dwell completion, but requires speaking "Click" to activate, eliminating accidental clicks (0.0% rate).

🎓 Presentation Viva Summary

  • Practical Feasibility: Handsfree navigation allows full web access without physical contact, achieving high reliability across testing tasks.
  • Head vs Hand Modalities: Head tracking offers continuous stability and minimal upper-limb fatigue for motor-impaired users, while Hand tracking provides natural gestural pointing for semi-mobile users.
  • Interaction Model: Continuous cursor control coupled with intentional discrete voice triggering eliminates the classic Midas Touch penalty.
  • Multi-Browser Compatibility: Built and verified for Chromium (Chrome, Brave, Edge) and Mozilla Firefox.
